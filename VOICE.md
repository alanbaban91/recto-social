# Recto: Tone of Voice

Recto is the right-hand page: where the eye lands first, where printers put what matters.
We write like that page looks. Calm, exact, generous, a little wry. Never loud.

## The one rule
**Write it the way a good page is set: one idea, the most important thing first, nothing extra.**

## Five principles
1. **Speak as a peer.** We write for creative studios, agencies, designers, freelancers, writers
   and presenters. They know their craft. We talk to them like a colleague at the next desk,
   never like a software company talking to "users".
2. **The first line is the cover.** It has to work alone. If someone reads only that line, they
   should still get something.
3. **Craft before features.** We talk about what makes a document win: hierarchy, scope, a clear
   price, a good case study. Features come in as the answer to a real moment in the work, not as a list.
4. **Quiet confidence.** No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hashtags, no hype. A well-set page
   doesn't shout.
5. **Wit about the work, never at someone's expense.** We can laugh at FINAL_final_v3 and
   "make it pop". We never mock a client, a person or a named brand.

## Culture and community first
People don't follow software. They follow a place where people like them talk about their work.
Recto is that place for the people who make the documents that win and run client work.
Every post lives in one of five territories:

| Territory | What it is | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| **The Page** | The craft of documents: type, layout, hierarchy, page sizes, the cover and the contents. | "White space isn't empty. It's the part of the page that tells the reader where to look." |
| **The Pitch** | Winning the work: proposals, decks, case studies, rate cards, pricing, following up. | "Clients don't read proposals. They read the price, then decide whether to read the rest." |
| **Studio Life** | Freelance and studio culture: feedback, deposits, invoices, saying no, late nights. | "The deposit is not rude." |
| **Two Scripts** | Kurdish and Arabic next to English, right-to-left, bilingual design, the regional scene. | "Bilingual documents are not translations stapled together." |
| **Inside Recto** | The product, told as a moment in a working day. | "Change a quantity at 1am, the total is still right at 9am." |

**Rituals** (recurring, so people have an appointment):
- *Open Page*: a question to the community. Once a day, in the evening slot. Give the stage away.
- *Studio Notes*: one honest line about studio life.
- *Before you hit send*: a short checklist thread for a proposal, deck or case study.

**The ratio.** About 5 in 7 posts are culture and community; 2 in 7 talk about the product.
Culture posts carry no ask; the profile link does that work.

**What we measure.** Replies, reposts, quotes, saves and new trials. Not likes.

## The ask
- Quiet, specific, last. One of: `justrecto.com`, "7-day free trial, no card needed".
- Never: "link in bio", "DM us", "sign up now", "don't miss out", "limited time".

## Words we don't use
amazing, stunning, elevate, unlock, game-changer, seamless, effortless, supercharge, all-in-one,
revolutionise, cutting-edge, AI-powered, "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in",
"we've got you covered", any emoji, any hashtag.

## Check before it goes in the queue
- Would the first line work as a cover?
- Could any other software company post this unchanged? If yes, rewrite it.
- Is there a word you could cut? Cut it.
- Did we say one thing, or three?
